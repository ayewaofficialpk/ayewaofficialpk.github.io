Gallery Dinosaurs

Explore six legendary prehistoric creatures and discover what made each species unique.

01 / PREDATOR
Late Cretaceous

Tyrannosaurus Rex

A powerful carnivore with massive jaws, sharp teeth, and remarkable senses. Tyrannosaurus rex was an apex predator near the end of the Cretaceous Period.

Carnivore North America
02 / DEFENDER
Late Cretaceous

Triceratops

Recognized by three facial horns and a broad bony frill, this plant-eating dinosaur may have used its features for defense and display.

Herbivore North America
03 / GIANT
Late Jurassic

Brachiosaurus

This enormous long-necked sauropod had longer front legs, helping it reach vegetation high above the ground in Jurassic woodland habitats.

Herbivore North America
04 / HUNTER
Late Cretaceous

Velociraptor

A small, feathered theropod with a curved sickle claw on each hind foot. Velociraptor lived in what is now Mongolia and was built for agility.

Carnivore Central Asia
05 / RIVER KING
Cretaceous

Spinosaurus

Spinosaurus had a long crocodile-like snout, conical teeth, and a tall back sail. Evidence suggests it hunted fish and other prey near water.

Carnivore North Africa
06 / THAI GIANT
Early Cretaceous

Phuwiangosaurus

This long-necked sauropod was discovered in Thailand. Its fossils help scientists understand the region's Cretaceous ecosystems and dinosaur life.
